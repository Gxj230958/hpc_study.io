# Other Competitions

**Prerequisites:** [Competition overview](competition-intro.md). **Objective:** Compare contests by requirements rather than labels.

Compare allowed hardware, power and cost constraints, application mix, correctness criteria and scoring. Distinguish student events, vendor demonstrations and formal benchmark submissions.

An archived result can illustrate a method without establishing a current ranking. Cite the edition and measurement conditions whenever reusing historical examples.

Next, design a small reproducible [benchmark](../../benchmark/intro.md) before adopting contest-specific tooling.

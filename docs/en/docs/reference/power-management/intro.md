# Power and Energy

**Prerequisites:** Timing and units. **Objective:** Distinguish power limits from energy-to-solution.

Power is energy per unit time, usually watts. Energy-to-solution integrates power over runtime. A lower instantaneous power can consume more total energy if the job takes much longer.

Thermal design power is a design envelope, not a measured energy reading. Whole-system energy includes CPUs, memory, cooling and other components beyond a single GPU sensor.

Do not change shared hardware power settings without allocation authority. This course uses read-only analysis. Next, examine [power measurement](perception.md).
